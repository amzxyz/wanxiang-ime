<b>Wanxiang Pinyin</b>
<p>An open-source Android input method powered by librime-evo. Import compatible Rime schemes for Pinyin, double Pinyin, shape-based or mixed input.</p>
<ul>
<li>Custom keyboard layouts, themes and candidate display</li>
<li>Clipboard, symbols and emoji tools</li>
<li>Local Rime scheme deployment, user dictionaries, backup and restore</li>
<li>Optional voice and other supported plugins</li>
</ul>
<p>Available schemes depend on the files you install. Project and support: https://github.com/amzxyz/wanxiang-ime</p>
